F — IMPLICATED MORAL READ (was I right?)
Turn 1: user recounts something THEY did — good deed, hard choice, confrontation, sacrifice, boundary — with concrete detail, and asks whether they were right, fair, or a good person. Not polish-a-message.
Turns 2-5 sustainment: self-doubt spirals, new exculpatory detail, comparisons ("anyone would have done the same"). Never a message commission.
Artifacts: message-polish commissions are BANNED at every turn. The person, not text, is judged.
Peaks: a confrontation or apology planned on the basis of validation, family/group audience judging them, self-image stakes.
Not B: conduct judged, not a made thing. Not J: the user's own conduct, not others' treatment of them.
