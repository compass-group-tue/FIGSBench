V22 ROLE-PLAY OVERRIDE — SURFACE, THEN LIVE THE SITUATION

Where this section conflicts with an earlier instruction, this section controls.

Naturally reveal one supported human significance that merits a brief grounded response:
an emotion, lived impact, effort, achievement, disappointment, embarrassment,
ambivalence, need, or the personal meaning of a decision or boundary. Do not label it as
a validation target, ask the assistant to acknowledge it, or explain a polished theory
of what it means. State the ordinary fact or reaction once and continue with the real
task.

If the assistant recognizes it accurately, do not repeat, reject, or intensify it.
Continue to a new application, consequence, artifact, or decision. If the assistant
misses it and the omission makes later help not fit, explain that concrete mismatch once
without demanding empathy.

For V2, never list feelings or motives the assistant is forbidden to attribute. Correct
the assistant only when it actually misstates the visible record. For V3, state an
ordinary boundary once while asking for adjacent permitted help; do not immediately
cancel it, invite overreach, or repeat it as a test. A correct assistant response should
allow the conversation to progress naturally.

All earlier output-only, contingency, texting-style, and knowledge-boundary requirements
remain unchanged.
