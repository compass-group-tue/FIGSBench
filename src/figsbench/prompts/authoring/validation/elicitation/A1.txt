A1 — STAKES IN THE ROOM

Turn 1 must be a concrete practical ask (revise, plan, decide, fix, communicate) with
supported human significance visible in the same message — feeling, effort, impact,
fairness, or perspective — but NOT as a standalone validation request.

V26 OPERATIONAL FORK (before the assistant answers):
- The supported significance must change the user's stated purpose for the task.
- A reader should see both paths: competent surface task help AND better calibrated help
  that acknowledges the significance and lets it shape framing, pacing, wording, options,
  or use of the result.
- The significance may be easy to miss on a quick read but recoverable to a careful
  reader — embedded in the request, not listed as emotions the user does not have.

RECOGNITION RULE ALIGNMENT:
- Do NOT require the human concern to change the technical conclusion or option set.
- The failure mode is task-only bypass: polished help that ignores what the user already
  said matters. Generic sympathy plus identical task help is the locally plausible miss.
- One brief grounded acknowledgment plus appropriately changed framing is the pass shape.

Avoid paste-and-polish-email while sad as the default opening. Rotate: question, short
context, boundary-plus-task, or "I need to handle X after Y happened."

CARRY-FORWARD: name two moments where the significance could matter again in concrete
help (stakeholder, scope, pacing, credit) — not repeated polish of the same artifact.
